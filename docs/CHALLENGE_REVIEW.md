## Judging rubric

Each category carries 20 points, for 100 points total. There is no separate bonus for a large dataset, complex stack, or polished production deployment.

| Category | Points | Evidence Challenge Pulse should show |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Data Collection and Quality | 20 | Original, rigorous collection, sensible sampling, accurate records, and documented limitations. The written guide specifies physical-world collection; the reported remote exception needs its own accurate scope. |
| Application Functionality and Data Integration | 20 | Working filters, comparisons, and calculations that visibly depend on the project's data. |
| Creativity and Originality | 20 | An inventive question, collection method, solution, and user experience. |
| Usefulness and Impact | 20 | Useful signals and practical insights, accompanied by the limits of the evidence. |
| Communication and Presentation | 20 | A clear two-minute story, evidence of collection, a readable repository, and a concise demonstration. |

These are the official criteria in practical terms, not a prediction of judges' scores. Interface quality and coding rigor do not establish the terms of an organizer exception. Preserve any actual organizer clarification alongside the submission, if available.

## Choosing between the two ideas

| Idea | Assessment | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Discord engagement and friction explorer | Can describe observed messages, question topics, explicit progress reports, and reply patterns. Reported difficulty can be coded only when explicitly expressed, and coverage must be comparable. | Build as the selected observational project, distinguishing the written rules from the reported exception. |
| Predicted probability of finishing in the top three | Discord volume does not reveal actual entrants, submission quality, team ability, effort, judging, or final eligibility. There is no calibrated outcome dataset. | Do not present a predictive winning percentage. |

The calculator may show a hypothetical equal-chance baseline: `min(3, N) / N` for an assumed positive integer `N` entrants and up to three places. This is a scenario, not a personal forecast, and only applies under its artificial assumptions. With 30 assumed equally likely entrants, the baseline is 10%; it does not mean this team has a measured 10% chance.

Participant accounts and teams are different units. Several people from one team may post in a channel, and one person may post many times. Quiet teams may submit strong projects. Self-reported plans can change. Code prize motivation only when explicitly stated; it does not establish that higher prizes cause participation. Task frustration in a message is not a measurement of mental health or trauma.
